# Get Piggus To The Toilet: Project Notes

A game made for my 8-year-old daughter. It runs by double-clicking an HTML file on her Windows laptop, with no installs. I'm not a programmer, so please explain things simply and keep changes in the same single HTML file.

## How the game is packaged
- Folder **PiggusAdventure** contains **Piggus-Adventure.html** (the game) and a **Resources** subfolder for my own pictures and sounds.
- Most artwork and music are built into the HTML file itself, so the file is about 23 MB.
- The checklist "What goes in Resources.txt" lists every optional file name the game looks for. Missing files fall back to built-in drawings, beeps or speech bubbles.
- Sea levels sped up on 2026-10-04 (about 8x faster to draw, same look): the underwater sway is drawn from one copy of the screen instead of 56, the water tint is painted once, the hidden sky is skipped, and sea pictures use the browser's quicker smoothing.
- Played only on a PC or laptop in a web browser, with the keyboard; never on tablets (James, 2026-10-04). The old tablet controls in the supermarket are still in the file but aren't worked on.
- Desktop shortcut: points at the HTML file, with Piggus.ico from Resources as the icon.
- When making changes: edit the latest HTML I attach, test it, and send back the whole file. Tell me if any new file names need adding to Resources.

## Characters
- **Piggus**: pink pig squishmallow, the hero. Has pictures facing front, back, left and right, and squishes when he hops.
- **Winston**: owl friend who pops up in corners with encouraging messages, hoots and wobbles.
- **Lady Squishshot**: hunter in a pith helmet and safari gear (three picture variations). Patrols lanes.
- **Dino-Capy**: Capybara, Piggus's nemesis, who wears a green dinosaur suit. The final boss.
- **Friends**: caged squishmallows to rescue in the Forest (friend1 frog, friend2 cat, room for up to 8).
- **Prison guards**: koala guard squishes and bulldog guard dogs.
- **Brock the Bat Dog**: bulldog squishmallow with bat wings. Swoops down at Piggus in the supermarket.

## Other features
- **Reaching the toilet**: Winston cheers while she mashes the space bar to fill a meter. Piggus shakes more as it fills, then the toilet flushes.
- **Squish Gem**: 1 in 5 chance per try. Gives a few seconds of glowing invincibility.
- **Buffet bonus round**: sometimes after a level. Catch sweets and cakes, avoid broccoli ("Ugh!", and it takes away 50 points, shown as a red -50 with the score box flashing red; the score never goes below 0; added 2026-10-04), space bar jumps, bakery background, best score saved. Once a round (sometimes twice) a sparkly iced bun with a cherry and a pink swirl drops: Winston's Special Cherry Fizz Swirl, worth 250 points (added 2026-10-03). It was made smaller later that day, and catching it just shows a gold +250. The Buffet start screen explains it.
- **Every level is checked** to have a safe route through before it starts.
- **Menu (Esc or ☰ button)**: carry on, restart level, start from level 1, go to any level, volume, music on/off, next song.
- **Master volume** (added 2026-10-03): the Volume line in the menu (◀ ▶ or tap either end) turns everything up or down in 10% steps: music, sound effects and voice lines. The game remembers it on that computer. MASTER_VOLUME at the top of the file is the starting volume (1 = full).
- **Instructions mention only the arrow keys and space bar** (no WASD).
- **Toilet space-bar meter** stops getting harder after level 8, so the later levels stay doable.
- **New optional sounds for Resources**: nooo.mp3 (Piggus getting hooked), gulp.mp3 (grabbing air), selfscan.mp3 (the "Piggus detected in the bagging area" voice at the checkouts), bacon.mp3, bacon2.mp3 and bacon3.mp3 (James's own voice lines when Piggus spots a bacon sign, e.g. "Argh!"; one at random, about half the time), chilly.mp3 (James's own "Ugh, chilly!" for the frozen food counter, about half the time), ooga.mp3 (a caveman's "Ooga booga!" in the Dino Jungle), roar.mp3 (a T-rex spotting Piggus), timego.mp3 and timehome.mp3 (Piggus's voice on the time travel there and back). Without them the game makes its own sounds and reads "Noooo!", the self-scan line and "Ugh, chilly!" out loud. beanfart.mp3 (the big fart when Piggus uses the beans) and beanjump.mp3 (a whoosh or boing for the giant bean jump), added 2026-10-04: without beanfart.mp3 the game uses toot.mp3, then a built-in noise; without beanjump.mp3 a built-in whoosh. toot.mp3 also plays when Piggus grabs a tin of beans. (takeoff.mp3 and landing.mp3 are no longer used.)
- **Built-in pictures added**: mummy, three shark poses (side, front, darting), crab (claws animated separately), clam. Copies with backgrounds removed are in game/pictures.
- **Supermarket pictures** (round 2, 2026-10-03): fruit-and-veg aisle with wooden table (Vecteezy watermarks and Pro badge removed; very faint traces may show on close inspection), toilet-roll trolley (flipped to face left), orange checkout, checkout with cashier cut from the staff picture, Fresh Meat counter and Frozen Food counter (round 4), wet floor sign, two bacon adverts, Special Offer sign, bomb trolley (flipped to face left, pushed by Lady Squishshot's net picture), bathroom picture for the finish, and Brock the Bat Dog (round 6; his wings were cut off as separate pictures so they can flap). - **Sharp on big screens**: the game still lays everything out on a 640 x 448 screen, but draws with as many pixels as the laptop shows it at (up to 1.2x, the SHARPNESS setting; James chose 1.2 on 2026-10-03, so keep it at 1.2 in future files).
- **Lady Squishshot pictures**: her three pictures were sharpened and smoothed (same colours and style), from the Lady Squishshot pictures thread.

The new ones were volume-matched to the originals (all about -10.7 LUFS, the same as the originals' average) and stored at 96 kbps like them, which makes the file about 22 MB. The game starts on a random song; N or the menu skips to the next. Extra songs can still go in Resources as song1.mp3 ... song9.mp3.

## Boss fight (level 15)
- **Silent intro**: Piggus walks in, "My toilet! Finally!" Capy on the toilet: "Not so fast, Piggus! This is MY toilet!" Piggus: "But you're just a little capybara!" Capy: "Well, I'm not a capybara… I'm a DINOSAUR!!" He climbs into the dino suit and roars. Space bar skips.
- **Fight**: boss music starts. 5 hearts each. He throws **toilet paper only**, and powers up ("Haaaaa!") then slams to send **blue electric bolts** along the floor. When he's tired, jump on his head to bop him.
- **Enraged on his last health**: turns red, steams, chases Piggus faster and attacks more. No big "ENRAGED!" text.
- **Ending**: defeated flat capybara: "Okay, okay… you win, Piggus! The toilet's all yours." Piggus sits on the toilet: "Ahhh… peace at last!" Finale reads "Peace at last! Piggus can finally poop in peace!"
- **Voice line file names** (mp3s in Resources) are listed in the checklist. Voices play at 75% volume (VOICE_VOLUME setting).
