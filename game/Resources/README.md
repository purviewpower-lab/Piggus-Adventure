# Resources folder

Put your own sounds (.mp3) and pictures here, next to `Piggus-Adventure.html`. When one is missing the game uses its built-in drawing, beep or spoken line instead. The file names must match exactly.

## Sounds

- **Hopping and eating:** hop1.mp3, hop2.mp3, hop3.mp3, munch1.mp3, munch2.mp3, munch3.mp3
- **Getting caught:** oops.mp3, oof.mp3, oof1.mp3 ... oof9.mp3, ugh.mp3, splash.mp3, alarm.mp3, hot.mp3, nooo.mp3
- **Winning:** win.mp3, winston.mp3
- **Beans and air:** toot.mp3, beanfart.mp3, beanjump.mp3, gulp.mp3
- **Supermarket:** bacon.mp3, bacon2.mp3, bacon3.mp3, chilly.mp3, selfscan.mp3
- **Dino Jungle:** ooga.mp3, roar.mp3, timego.mp3, timehome.mp3
- **Dino voice lines (optional, 1 to 3 of each):** rex1-3 (T-rex spots Piggus, half the time), raptor1-3 (a raptor leaps out, 1 in 4), ooga1-3 (caveman throws, 1 in 3, ooga.mp3 counts as one), ptero1-3 (a pterodactyl near Piggus in the volcano section, 1 in 3), bronto1-3 (a long neck swinging into view, 1 in 3), dino1-3 (Piggus lands on a dino, 1 in 5). One at a time, at least 6 seconds apart.
- **Extra music:** song1.mp3 ... song9.mp3 are added to the playlist
- **Boss voice lines:** piggus_mytoilet, capy_notsofast, piggus_littlecapy, capy_dinosaur, capy_roar, winston_bop, capy_powerup, capy_tired1-3, capy_ow1-5, piggus_ouch1-3, capy_laugh, capy_youwin, winston_youdidit, piggus_peace (each as .mp3)
